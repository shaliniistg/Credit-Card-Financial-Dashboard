# Business Insights

## Executive Summary

The dashboard analyzes approximately ₹57 million in revenue generated through over 667K credit card transactions. The analysis identifies key customer segments, spending behavior, payment trends, and card performance using interactive Power BI dashboards connected to a MySQL database.

---

## Revenue Performance

- Total Revenue: ₹57M
- Week-over-Week Revenue Growth: 28.8%
- Total Transaction Amount: ₹45.5M
- Total Transactions: 667K
- Total Interest Earned: ₹8M

---

## Card Performance

- Blue Cards generated ₹47M, contributing nearly 83% of total revenue.
- Silver Cards ranked second with ₹5.66M.

---

## Spending Pattern

- Bills generated the highest revenue (₹14M).
- Entertainment ranked second (₹10M).
- Chip-enabled transactions accounted for over 70% of all transactions.

---

## Quarterly Trend

- Q4 recorded the highest overall performance among all quarters.

---

## Customer Analysis

- Male customers generated ₹31M while female customers generated ₹26M.
- Customers aged 40–50 years generated the highest revenue across both genders.
- High-income male customers generated the highest revenue among males.
- Low-income female customers generated the highest revenue among female income groups.
- Graduate customers generated the highest revenue across both genders.
- Married customers generated the highest revenue.
- Texas, New York and California were the highest revenue-generating states.

---

## Recommendations

- Focus customer acquisition on customers aged 40–50 years.
- Increase adoption of Silver, Gold and Platinum cards.
- Promote offers related to Bills and Entertainment spending.
- Continue encouraging chip-enabled transactions.
- Launch targeted marketing campaigns in Texas, New York and California.
